# Personal Intelligence Refinery

## Governed AI-assisted research and decision support

External material can be useful without being trustworthy enough to become system truth. Personal Intelligence Refinery (PIR) is a private, local-first system designed to turn untrusted material into grounded, reviewable decision support while retaining deterministic controls and human authority.

Its governing distinction is simple:

> A model may propose a CandidateClaim. It cannot create a durable Claim.

PIR creates a controlled corridor:

```text
External material
→ extracted source evidence
→ model proposes CandidateClaim
→ exact grounding + deterministic validation
→ human accepts / edits / rejects / defers
→ only approved material may become a durable Claim
```

This public work sample is a curated interpretation layer. The canonical PIR repository, source material, and operational records remain private.

## What was proven

A bounded V008 validation used five frozen, public-safe synthetic fixtures to test whether a hosted model could reduce manual claim-identification work without bypassing grounding, validation, cost, privacy, or human-review boundaries.

- **5 / 5** expected material spans covered
- **0** grounding violations
- **5 ACCEPT / 1 REJECT** human decisions
- **YES** operator judgment that manual claim-identification work was reduced

The $0.002041 hosted-model spend was reconciled against a one-time $0.05 experiment ceiling. This was a bounded validation harness, not routine hosted inference or a production provider integration.

## What this does not claim

The evidence does not prove autonomous research, automatic fact verification, full evidence adjudication, routine hosted inference, enterprise deployment, or general prompt-injection resistance. The public examples are synthetic; no private research cases, source captures, or operational data are included.
